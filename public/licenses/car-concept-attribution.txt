Source model originally derived from Unity Fan's public-domain concept car.

Carnage X-Ray adaptations: material styling, hiding logo/license meshes and the
illustrative engine mesh, plus pointer-driven part translations and separation.
The GLB source bytes are retained unchanged; all adaptations occur at runtime.
This is an exterior design study, not validated CAD or a model of every
educational component. No author endorsement is implied.
